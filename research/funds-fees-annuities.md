- **Allan Gray:** tiered administration fee about 0.50% on the first R1.5m, 0.20% on the next R3.5m, 0.10% above R5m; Allan Gray's own unit trusts carry no separate platform fee (the management fee covers administration).

Fee-impact evidence:

- National Treasury's 2013 discussion paper *Charges in South African Retirement Funds* found that a regular saver whose charges fall from **2.5% to 0.5% of assets p.a. ends with roughly 60% more at retirement after 40 years** (equivalently, 2.5% instead of 0.5% leaves about 40% less). This is the standard citation used by 10X and ASISA ([Treasury paper](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2013/2013071101%20-%20Charges%20in%20South%20African%20Retirement%20Funds.pdf); URL not fetched in-session, medium confidence). Our own reproduction (10% gross return, level contributions, 40 years) gives 73% more / 42% less, bracketing Treasury's figure.
- Computed in-session (10% gross, one extra percentage point of fee): a lump sum ends **8.7% lower after 10 years, 16.7% after 20, 24.0% after 30, 30.6% after 40**; a regular saver ends 17.9% lower after 30 years and **24.4% lower after 40 years** — consistent with the widely quoted "1% extra fee ≈ 20–27% less retirement income over 40 years".
- Living-annuity income: with a 4% gross real return, every extra 1 pp of fee cuts the level real income sustainable for 25 years by ~11% (1%→2% fee) and ~21% (1%→3% fee); over 30 years by ~12.5% and ~24%.

### 1.3 Living-annuity rules (2026/27)

- Income must be drawn at **between 2.5% and 17.5%** of the capital value per year; the rate (and frequency: monthly, quarterly, annually) can be changed **once a year on the policy anniversary** ([10X FAQ](https://www.10x.co.za/living-annuity-faq), [Just SA guide](https://justsa.co.za/individual-investor/living-annuity-guide-south-africa/), [Investonline 2026 guide](https://www.investonline.co.za/insights/how-living-annuities-work-2026-guide-to-drawdown-fees-and-risk/)).
- Industry data (ASISA, 2025): living-annuity assets **R911.7 billion**; average drawdown **6.6% in 2025**, down from 7.0% in 2011 ([FAnews/ASISA](https://www.fanews.co.za/article/compliance-regulatory/2/association-for-savings-investment-sa-asisa/1276/living-annuity-assets-grow-to-r911-7-bn-in-2025-with-annual-drawdown-rates-steady-at-6-6/44631)). Research cited by 10X and Investonline puts the **sustainable range at 4%–5%** ([10X blog](https://www.10x.co.za/blog/drawdown-rates-and-your-living-annuity), [Investonline](https://www.investonline.co.za/insights/whats-a-safe-living-annuity-drawdown-rate/)).
- Income is taxed as ordinary income at marginal rates via PAYE; no income tax, CGT or dividends tax inside the policy ([10X](https://www.10x.co.za/faq/how-is-a-living-annuity-taxed)).
- Transfer into a living annuity at retirement, and between insurers, is tax-free (SARS directive + ROT) ([SARS ROT guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-to-complete-submit-and-cancel-a-recognition-of-transfer/)).
- A living annuity may be fully commuted to cash if its value is below **R150 000 from 1 March 2026** (R125 000 before) ([Sanlam key values](https://seb-news.sanlam.co.za/consultant-toolkit/key-retirement-fund-values-and-changes-effective-1-march-2026/)).
- Death: paid to **nominated beneficiaries**, who may take cash (taxed on the retirement lump-sum table in the deceased's hands) or continue the annuity; living annuities are **excluded from estate-duty "property"** under s3(2)(i) of the Estate Duty Act and attract no executor's fees ([Tax Faculty](https://taxfaculty.ac.za/news/read/estate-duty-living-annuities), [Allan Gray death-claim guide](https://www.allangray.co.za/globalassets/documents-repository/product/brochures/Living%20Annuity/Files/Understanding%20the%20death%20claim%20process%20for%20the%20Allan%20Gray%20Living%20Annuity.pdf)).
- **Regulation 28 does not apply** to living annuities, so up to 100% offshore exposure is possible via rand-denominated feeder funds (provider-dependent) and a living annuity can be **converted to a guaranteed life annuity at any time (one-way)** — medium confidence; these are standard provider statements not confirmed by an in-session snippet ([10X FAQ](https://www.10x.co.za/living-annuity-faq), [Just SA](https://justsa.co.za/individual-investor/living-annuity-guide-south-africa/)).
- Minimum amount to buy: provider-specific; not found (open question).

### 1.4 Preservation-fund rules (2026/27)

- On retirement from a pension/pension-preservation fund at most **one-third** may be taken in cash; two-thirds must buy an annuity; the whole benefit may be taken in cash if the total retirement interest is below **R360 000 from 1 March 2026** (R247 500 before) ([SARS Tax and Retirement](https://www.sars.gov.za/individuals/tax-during-all-life-stages-and-events/tax-and-retirement/), [Sanlam key values](https://seb-news.sanlam.co.za/consultant-toolkit/key-retirement-fund-values-and-changes-effective-1-march-2026/)).
- Retirement from a preservation fund is allowed from **age 55** (earlier only on ill-health) — medium confidence (Income Tax Act definition of "retirement date"; SARS page above).
- Two-pot (from 1 September 2024): the **vested component keeps its pre-existing right to one withdrawal before retirement** (taxed on the withdrawal table); the **savings component** may be withdrawn once per tax year (minimum R2 000, taxed at marginal rates); the **retirement component** cannot be accessed before retirement and must be annuitised ([SARS two-pot](https://www.sars.gov.za/two-pot-retirement-system/), [Treasury FAQ Aug 2024](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/2024%20Two-pot%20System%20Updated%20%20FAQ%20August%202024.pdf), [Glacier](https://www.glacierinsights.co.za/blog/retirement-insights/two-pot-retirement-system-upon-resignation)).
- **Regulation 28** (amended, effective 3 January 2023): **75% equity, 25% property, 45% offshore** (Africa included in the 45%), 15% private equity, 45% infrastructure, 0% crypto assets — medium confidence; the 45% offshore limit was announced in the February 2022 Budget and gazetted in the 2022 amendments (URL from memory: [Treasury Budget Review 2022](https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/national%20budget/2022/review/FullBR.pdf)).
- A resigning GEPF member may transfer the actuarial interest tax-free to a pension preservation fund; the GEPF's retirement component must go to the retirement component of the receiving fund (see `research/gepf-benefit-rules.md`).

### 1.5 Guaranteed life-annuity indicative rates

- **Glacier by Sanlam, June 2026** (the only 2026 quote found): single-life, **age 65, 5% annual escalation, 10-year guarantee, R5 million** premium → **R28 443/month gross (male)** and **R25 648/month (female)**. Per **R1 million: R5 689 (male) / R5 130 (female) per month**, i.e. an initial yield of 6.83% / 6.16% ([Glacier Sanlam life annuity page](https://www.glacier.co.za/personal/retirement/retirementincome/Pages/sanlam-life-annuity.aspx)). Medium-high confidence (snippet of the provider page).
- A Smart About Money article ("Guaranteed pensions offering best rates in a decade") quotes **R7 117/month for a 65-year-old man on R1 million, "increasing at 6% a year"**; the article date and exact terms could not be confirmed and the figure is inconsistent with the Glacier quote (it would imply an 8.5% initial yield with 6% escalation), so it is recorded at **low** confidence ([Smart About Money](https://smartaboutmoney.co.za/hot-topics/guaranteed-pensions-offering-best-rates-in-a-decade/)).
- Rates move with long-bond yields and differ by insurer (Sanlam, Just SA, Momentum, Old Mutual, Liberty) ([Sanlam: factors affecting annuity pricing](https://seb-news.sanlam.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Factors-affecting-annuity-pricing.pdf), [Masthead rates](https://www.masthead.co.za/annuity-investment-rates/)).
- **Not found (low-confidence priors only):** level and CPI-linked quotes at 60 and 65. Basis for the priors: SA nominal long-bond yields of roughly 10–11% and index-linked real yields of roughly 4.5–5% in 2025–26, consistent with the 6.8% initial yield on the Glacier 5%-escalating quote. Indicative per R1m, male, single life: **age 65 — level ~R9 200–10 000/month, CPI-linked ~R5 500–6 200/month, 5% escalating ~R5 500–5 900/month; age 60 — level ~R8 300–9 000, CPI-linked ~R4 800–5 400, 5% escalating ~R5 000–5 400**. Female rates roughly 8–10% lower. **Must be re-verified with live quotes.**
- For valuing a GEPF-style CPI-linked pension the app should use the CPI-linked rate; the Glacier 5%-escalating figure (R5 689/m per R1m at 65) is the closest verified proxy.

### 1.6 Hybrid / with-profit annuities and the SA "4% rule" research

- Sustainable-drawdown consensus: 4–5% ([10X](https://www.10x.co.za/blog/drawdown-rates-and-your-living-annuity), [Investonline](https://www.investonline.co.za/insights/whats-a-safe-living-annuity-drawdown-rate/)); ASISA average is 6.6% ([FAnews/ASISA](https://www.fanews.co.za/article/compliance-regulatory/2/association-for-savings-investment-sa-asisa/1276/living-annuity-assets-grow-to-r911-7-bn-in-2025-with-annual-drawdown-rates-steady-at-6-6/44631)).
- FAnews (2026, "More levers for retirement planners to pull") discusses new blended/hybrid annuity levers ([FAnews](https://www.fanews.co.za/article/retirement/1357/general/1358/more-levers-for-retirement-planners-to-pull/44610)); content not extracted.
- Prior knowledge (low confidence, not verified in-session): Just SA's **Lifetime Income** with-profit annuity can be blended inside living annuities on several platforms (Allan Gray since 2019/20, Sygnia, Glacier, Momentum), Sanlam/Glacier offer with-profit and hybrid "investment-linked lifetime income" options, and Old Mutual, Momentum and Liberty offer with-profit annuities whose increases depend on a bonus declaration; Allan Gray's and Just SA's published research both conclude that drawdowns above ~5% carry a high probability of real income falling over a 30-year retirement, and that annuitising part of the capital (a hybrid) raises sustainable income for the same longevity protection.

### 1.7 Offshore allocation in living annuities; USD products

- Because Regulation 28 does not apply, living annuities at the major LISPs (Allan Gray, Sygnia, 10X, Ninety One, Glacier, Momentum, Old Mutual Wealth, Discovery) can hold up to **100% in rand-denominated offshore feeder funds**, subject to the insurer's own limits and available foreign capacity — medium confidence, provider statements not confirmed in-session.
- A living annuity is a policy of a South African long-term insurer and is **rand-denominated**; some platforms (e.g. Ninety One, Glacier International, Momentum Wealth International) allow the underlying assets to be held directly in foreign-currency funds via the insurer's asset-swap capacity, and the income can be paid in rand only. No genuinely USD-denominated living annuity product was verified. Open question.
